# UX review 2: real rollup flow

I inspected all nine `rollup-*-1440x900.png` desktop captures and both `rollup-phone-*-390x844.png` captures, then compared them with `docs/UX-REVIEW-1.md` and `docs/BUILD-NOTES.md`. I also checked the current pulse, privacy-check, and witness-rail code where the truth of an on-screen claim depends on implementation. This is a review of local captures and source, not a public-network timing study or independent security audit. The on-chain fill, persistence, and account reads are a substantial functional step. The first glance still reads as an instrument panel with its main instrument unavailable.

## 2. Ranked remaining weaknesses, with exact fixes

1. **The largest panel says the market has no history.** `rollup-01-arrival-1440x900.png`, `rollup-03-market-long-filled-1440x900.png`, and `rollup-07-spot-filled-1440x900.png`, center x200-900 y177-537: an empty chart consumes about 360 px of height while the tape underneath proves the feed has prices. Fix the real `/v1/candles` history or candle initialization so valid OHLCV renders on arrival. Until there are two real candles, render a clearly labelled live mark and public-trade plot derived from actual events in that same area, with a `History building` label and sample count. Keep the unavailable message only for an actual fetch failure. Do not synthesize candles.
2. **The privacy pass can be false on a failed read.** `rollup-08-privacy-check-1440x900.png`, `On-chain accounts` card x908-1120 y445-644: two green `empty` results look conclusive. In `src/lib/trading/rollupClient.ts`, both `getAccountInfo(...).catch(() => null)` calls make transport or RPC errors indistinguishable from a genuinely absent response. Return a three-way result for each address: `not returned`, `returned`, or `check failed`; fail the check on a thrown request and never color it green. Display network, checked time, shortened account addresses, and `Unsigned RPC read` alongside the result. This is a claim integrity fix before any investor demo.
3. **The visible latency numbers invite the wrong comparison.** `rollup-03-market-long-filled-1440x900.png`, pulse x908-1120 y120-183 and rail y347-382: `p50 5 ms / p99 56 ms / n=208` sits next to `30 ms` with no immediate `local` qualifier. Build notes identify the stack as loopback; the user reports about 0.5 s on the public test network from Europe. Put `LOCAL DEMO` in the pulse itself and label its metric `venue accept/reject`, not end-to-end fill. For investor presentation, lead with a separately measured public-network click-to-result number and its location/sample; keep local diagnostics in a detail view. Wording is specified in section 4.
4. **Receipt chronology is impossible as displayed.** `rollup-03-market-long-filled-1440x900.png`, witness rail x920-1108 y275-340: `Submitted 21:45:45.747`, `Confirmed 21:45:45.777`, then `Filled 21:45:45.000`. `rollup-05-open-orders-1440x900.png` similarly shows `Resting 21:45:45.207` after a `21:45:46.041` submit. These appear to mix rounded venue event timestamps with precise local timestamps. Use one clock and precision for a chronological rail, or omit the absolute fill/resting time and label a venue timestamp separately. Never display a later stage as occurring before submission.
5. **Market-specific evidence follows the previous trade.** `rollup-07-spot-filled-1440x900.png`, selected `NSOL-NUSD` at top and tape `yours` x200-900, but witness rail x908-1120 still says `Long 1.000 SOL · Market, max buy 109.6 nUSD` from the prior perp order. Key the rail by market and show the spot buy receipt when spot is selected. Preserve a cross-market recent activity control so switching markets does not erase history. `rollup-09-after-reload-1440x900.png` shows that the rail currently reverts to an empty prompt after reload despite the persisted position; recover the last known outcome or explicitly label the rail `This session only` and link to fills.
6. **A resting order is hard to act on.** `rollup-05-open-orders-1440x900.png`, dock x16-1424 y675-855 and rail x908-1120 y190-422: the row contains market, side, type, price, remaining size, but no visible order ID or `Cancel` button. The rail stops at `Remaining 0.200 SOL of 0.200`; its own control is below the internal fold. Put `Cancel #<id>` at the row's right edge, show ID and age there, and keep a visible rail cancel in the pinned bottom of that panel. The result in `rollup-06-cancelled-1440x900.png` should name the cancelled ID and size.
7. **The account dock is visible but far too sparse.** `rollup-04-position-1440x900.png`, x16-1424 y675-855: one row has `NSOL-PERP / long / 1.000 / 108.7` and leaves most of 180 px blank. A daily trader needs mark, unrealized P&L, collateral or margin, liquidation estimate if defensible, and close or reduce controls. Use the dock's width for those columns; if the service cannot provide a trustworthy figure, show `Unavailable` with a reason, not a fabricated zero. In `rollup-07-spot-filled-1440x900.png`, the dock remains on `Open orders / No open orders` and never surfaces the spot holding just acquired. Switch to `Balances` or show an immediate `Bought 1.000 SOL` result with the updated spot balance.
8. **The phone screenshots expose an unstable viewport hierarchy.** `rollup-phone-funded-390x844.png`, top of visible form y176, starts at a clipped `Max buy price` label; `rollup-phone-filled-390x844.png`, right before witness rail y444, cuts the form at the price field. Even if these were captured mid-scroll, the sticky header plus long form means the active control and result are separated. Add a 48 px sticky order summary/action bar with side, market, quantity, and current status; collapse shortcuts and secondary evidence on phone, keep result and position accessible from that bar, and make the first viewport show side, quantity, available balance, and primary action together.
9. **The claim proof is both small and overbroad.** `rollup-08-privacy-check-1440x900.png`, x908-1120 bottom: the green result is 11 px text under two generic `Check` buttons, followed by `should never expose`. A single unsigned read from this RPC at one moment supports only `account data not returned in this check`. Enlarge the two pass/fail rows, show checked time and endpoints or addresses, and place the scope caveat directly under them. Also state that public fills and timing remain visible; the check does not prove a trader cannot be correlated by other means.
10. **Finish details still say prototype.** `rollup-01-arrival-1440x900.png`, top market strip: the mark correctly displays `108.5` at the 0.1 tick, but `Vol 94.80 nUSD` and `OI 0.643 SOL` compete with it despite being minor context. `rollup-phone-funded-390x844.png`, market bar instead shows `108.500000` and footer wraps raw `tick 0.100000 · lot 0.001000 · max leverage 10x` across the bottom. `rollup-05-open-orders-1440x900.png` exposes `0.00` as the blank limit price in an untouched form. Format mark to tick (`108.5` here) consistently across phone and desktop, show size to lot only where editing or auditing requires it, use a blank price placeholder, and move raw contract metadata into a market details control. Remove the TradingView logo from the center of an unavailable chart in `rollup-01-arrival-1440x900.png` when no series is drawn.

## 3. Three investor-demo changes

1. **Make execution the hero while history is unavailable.** Reallocate the dead x200-900 chart canvas in `rollup-03-market-long-filled-1440x900.png` to a real trade-print plot plus a large latest receipt: `Bought 1.000 SOL · 3 fills · avg 108.8 nUSD` (compute the weighted average from actual fills, not this rounded example), with `30 ms local click to venue response` below it. Keep the compact mark and tape visible. When candles work, restore the full chart and put the receipt in a clear overlay or adjacent rail.
2. **Show privacy evidence at readable scale.** Move the two unsigned account-read results out of the 212 px right column during the demo into a 300-360 px evidence panel or a focused inspector alongside the public tape. Show `Trader view: no data returned`, `Market book: no data returned`, checked time, public RPC, and the two shortened addresses. Cut the explanatory paragraph and the duplicated generic `Check` labels. Use `Check public tape` and `Check unsigned accounts`.
3. **Use one representative public-network speed number.** In the market header beside `TEST NETWORK`, show `Europe to public test network · ~0.5 s observed click to result` only when an actual recorded run supports it; add sample size, last run, and exact start/end definition in a detail control. Put the local `p50 5 ms` inside `Local demo diagnostics`, not in the primary pitch. This makes the half-second claim intelligible and prevents the 5 ms loopback result from being mistaken for it.

## 4. Speed and privacy: wording and placement

**Speed today.** The top-right card in `rollup-03-market-long-filled-1440x900.png` says `p50 5 ms · p99 56 ms / n=208 · just now`; the witness rail says `This device, click to venue response: 30 ms`. Per the pulse code, p50/p99 are server time from venue arrival to first accept-or-reject response, including accepted and rejected orders. The individual 30 ms starts at this device's click and ends at the venue result. Neither figure is a public-test-network fill percentile. The build notes measure 30 local market orders at median 33 ms and p95 50 ms on one machine. Keep the live card honest with `LOCAL DEMO · venue response p50 5 ms · p99 56 ms · n=208`, then `Accept/reject, server clock · updated just now` and `How measured`. In the rail, use `This order · local click to result 30 ms`; place `Filled` and execution price separately. On a public deployment, replace the header speed line with `EUROPE → PUBLIC TEST NETWORK · ~0.5 s observed click to result`, plus a linked sample count and timestamp. The supplied ~0.5 s is an approximate observation, not a p50 or guarantee; do not convert it into one until measured.

**Privacy today.** `rollup-08-privacy-check-1440x900.png` shows the trader view and the market book as `empty` on unsigned reads. That is more concrete than a `private orders` badge, and the public tape remains visible. First fix failed RPC reads being counted as empty. Then, in the same right-side evidence area, use heading `Unsigned account check` and two large rows: `Trader view · no data returned` and `NSOL-NUSD book · no data returned`. Below them put `Public rollup RPC · checked 21:45:46 · view <short address> · book <short address>` using actual values. A footer link `What this shows` can open: `These two account reads returned no data without sign-in at the checked time. Public trade prints remain visible. This check does not establish anonymity or rule out timing correlation.` Do not say `orders are invisible to everyone`, `anonymous`, or `untraceable`. The current `should never expose` sentence should be removed from the result state because this check cannot prove a universal property.

## 5. Prototype tells and verification limits

- The giant `Price history unavailable` state, empty witness rail after reload, and previous-perp receipt on a spot screen look like unfinished data wiring, not a deliberate exchange interface. See `rollup-09-after-reload-1440x900.png` and `rollup-07-spot-filled-1440x900.png`.
- The footer in `rollup-09-after-reload-1440x900.png` says `account not yet synced` even while the dock shows a position and the order form shows a balance. A status label contradicting visible data is especially damaging in a financial UI.
- `TEST NETWORK`, a browser-generated test wallet, and `nUSD` correctly signal a test venue. They should stay. The word `terminal` in the logo, raw lot/tick metadata in the footer, and the TradingView mark on an error canvas make the surface feel like an engineering harness rather than a finished product.
- The screenshots prove a real local flow was captured, but cannot establish public-network latency distribution, privacy against all observers, liquidation accuracy, or behavior during disconnect. The first-order review, invalid order, partial fill, and network-down states are not present in this rollup set. Capture those before calling the corresponding review-1 items closed.
