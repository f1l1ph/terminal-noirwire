Spend the two-hour pass on visible contradictions and the recorded path.

## 2. MUST FIX for this recorded demo, ranked

1. **Make market freshness and connection status agree.** `devnet-08-spot-filled-1440x900.png`, market header x16-1424, left market list x16-191, order form x1128-1424: the header says `Unavailable · stale 14s`, the list still quotes 109.7 at `26s`, the form says wait for a fresh mark, yet the global dot says `Live`. `devnet-10-after-reload-1440x900.png` repeats it at 19 to 21 seconds. Keep stale marks blocked for new orders. Change the global label to `Connected · market data stale` when the selected mark exceeds the 10-second limit; replace stale list quotes with a muted `Last 109.7 · 26s ago` or `Unavailable`, not a current-looking price. Make the chart's last-mark line visibly historical. For recording, wait for a genuinely fresh mark and a working order button before each trade. Do not paper over a stale feed by raising the safety threshold.
2. **Remove the impossible funding action and spent-grant warning.** `devnet-01-arrival-1440x900.png`, right bottom: an already connected wallet with no available funds is offered `Get 5,000 test nUSD`; `devnet-03-funded-1440x900.png` and `devnet-04-market-long-1440x900.png`, under the disabled button: `This wallet already received its one-time grant.` Gate the grant CTA by actual grant eligibility. For a wallet that has used it, show `Grant used · 4,499.68 nUSD available` in account/funding details, and make the primary form action `Enter quantity`; never leave a yellow funding failure under trade submit. If eligibility cannot be checked up front, offer `Check test funds` with a clear result, not a repeat grant action. The phone capture `devnet-phone-filled-390x844.png` needs the same cleanup.
3. **Stop letting the p99 headline define the speed story.** `devnet-04-market-long-1440x900.png`, x908-1120 y120-211: `DEVNET · venue response p50 1.07 s · p99 12.91 s · n=500` is the first evidence card; the 12.91 s reads like the venue's normal order speed. Use the primary card for `This order · click to result 1.63 s` after the trade, with the start/end definition beside it; before a trade say `Devnet order timing appears after a result`. Move the venue distribution behind `Venue timing details`, where p50, p99, n, measured-from time, and the fact it covers accept/reject are all retained. Never call 1.63 s fill latency or say sub-second. The separate 20-order median in build notes must not be presented as an on-screen result unless the video shows its measurement.
4. **Put the selected spot fill on screen.** `devnet-08-spot-filled-1440x900.png`, witness rail x908-1120 y219-431 and dock x16-1424 y675-855: the page is on `NSOL-NUSD`, tape rows say `yours`, but the rail foregrounds `NSOL-PERP · cancelled` and the dock shows only a perp position. On a selected market's new fill, pin the latest receipt for that market above older activity and keep its Filled time, size, average price, and this-order duration in the visible rail. Select Balances in the dock or add a compact `Bought X SOL · balance Y SOL` row computed from the account read. Do not make the viewer infer the purchase from tiny `yours` pills.
5. **Make the first-minute account story coherent.** `devnet-01-arrival-1440x900.png` and `devnet-02-signed-in-1440x900.png` are visually identical, including wallet address; `devnet-03-funded-1440x900.png` already has a 4.700 SOL position before the recorded 1.000 SOL long. Use one pre-funded wallet and open the video on the funded screen, labeling it `Existing devnet test account`; or capture a genuinely new, eligible wallet and its grant flow. Do not narrate the unchanged signed-in capture as a sign-in or the existing position as the result of the next order. If using the existing wallet, show the position grows from 4.700 to 5.700 SOL as the precise outcome.
6. **Make the resting-order example credible and easy to cancel.** `devnet-06-resting-1440x900.png`, witness rail x908-1120 y219-431: `Long 0.100 SOL · Limit 66.5 nUSD` against a 109.7 mark looks like a test fixture, even though `Cancel order 3693` now works. Record a limit near the live market but outside the spread, within the venue's price band, and show the full `Open orders` dock row with ID, limit, remaining size, and `Cancel #ID`. Cancel that exact row, then hold on `devnet-07-cancelled-1440x900.png` long enough to read the cancelled ID and size. Avoid a forced far-away price chosen solely to make the script deterministic.
7. **Make the privacy evidence readable at video size.** `devnet-09-privacy-check-1440x900.png`, x908-1120 y326-666: the two green lines and checked time are roughly 11 px and sit under a stale-market warning. Provide a focused evidence state or panel at least 320 px wide with `Trader view: no data returned`, `NSOL-NUSD book: no data returned`, `Unsigned devnet rollup RPC`, check time, shortened addresses, and the actual endpoint accessible. Put one visible sentence immediately below: `These two unsigned reads returned no account data at this time. Public fills and timing remain visible.` Keep the three-way failed-read state; green must still require successful RPC responses.
8. **Do not let long-form account and receipt content clip at the shot boundary.** `devnet-07-cancelled-1440x900.png`, rail lower edge around y430: `Cancelled order 3693 · 0.100 remaining` is visibly cut. `devnet-08-spot-filled-1440x900.png` cuts the Filled time; `devnet-phone-filled-390x844.png` cuts the fill details at the fold. Reserve a fixed visible result block at the top of the rail and move old receipts into an internal scroll area. On phone, put the result summary immediately after submit, above the longer witness history. Record desktop for the main video if phone work cannot fit this pass.
